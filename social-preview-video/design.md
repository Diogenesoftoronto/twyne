# The writer's desk

Use the existing Twyne social preview as the design source. A quiet desk gives
the writer room to begin; gentle physical movement animates the scene while
the identity stays steady.

Palette: ivory paper `#faf3df`, ink `#1f1b16`, griffin/wordmark `#9f1c1f`, footer
`#4a3f33`. Preserve the natural warmth and leaf shadows in the photograph.

Typography: embedded Libre Baskerville regular / bold and DM Sans regular,
with exactly the sizing and placement authored in the shared SVG overlay.

Layout: 1200 × 630. Griffin and wordmark are the upper-left anchor; the two-line
headline is the focal element on the left; annotated manuscripts, pens, and
coffee balance it on the right; `twyne.love` anchors the lower left. Keep all
foreground text fixed throughout the clip.

Motion: generate one six-second shot with Seedance 2.5 through fal. Lock the
overhead camera, framing and scale. Coffee steam curls, flower sprigs sway
slightly and soft leaf shadows flutter. Preserve all other objects and the
clear ivory paper on the left. Supply the same photograph as the first and
last frame to encourage a smooth return.

HyperFrames plays the generated video as a native timed media clip, with the
shared SVG branding fixed above it. Do not add camera transforms or CSS motion.

The photographic scene provides the texture and depth. Keep the existing
lettering, copy, and warm daylight. The clip is deliberately silent.

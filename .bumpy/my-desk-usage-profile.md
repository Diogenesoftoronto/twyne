Twyne 0.18 gives every writer a private, local-first desk for understanding
their work, their editorial activity, and the cost of AI assistance.

**My Desk turns usage into a writing history.** A new responsive workspace
brings together active days, current folios, editorial actions, recent work,
deterministic writing patterns, and accessible charts. Signed-out writers keep
the full experience on this device; signed-in writers can synchronize it across
devices without making PostHog or manuscript content the source of truth.

**AI cost is legible without pretending estimates are bills.** The ledger
separates actual hosted cost, versioned provider estimates, local inference,
unknown cost, token dimensions, providers, models, features, and folios. Writers
can inspect the methodology, export JSON or CSV, clear local history, and delete
synchronized records.

**Public profiles remain private by default.** Writers can explicitly share a
small set of writing statistics while tokens, cost, models, private folios, and
usage patterns stay private. Version history, account deletion, authentication
analytics, provider diagnostics, and application error recovery are also more
dependable across the writing room.

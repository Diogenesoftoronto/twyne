Twyne 0.17 files the dossier as a working paper beside the manuscript and makes
the path from brief to desk easier to trust.

**The dossier now behaves like one document.** Creation and refinement share a
two-leaf folio with a complete live preview, local-first Particulars, embedded
draft review, and a clear filed acknowledgment before the editor opens. The
latest filed copy replaces the previous dossier paper in the active folio's
drawer.

**Writers can name the form without fighting a select box.** Essay remains the
default, a broad filterable list covers familiar forms, and free text remains
available for anything more specific.

**Public and collaborative state is more dependable.** The sitemap can include
published Convex pages automatically, provider review failures fall back and
report accurately, and comment resolution converges on the writer's requested
state instead of toggling out of sync.

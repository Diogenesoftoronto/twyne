Twyne 0.16 moves editorial help onto the manuscript and turns the Apparatus
into a fact-checker.

**The Editorial Board replaces the panel rails.** Citations, comments, the
rubric, and the personas now share one board that sits beside the draft, with
unread counts on each context and a single close key. Its panels stay mounted
while the board is shut, so a search, a stream, or a half-written reply
survives being put away.

**Work happens from the passage, not from a sidebar.** Selecting text offers
the three things a writer actually wants there — find sources for this, put a
note in the margin, send it to an editor — and marginalia, comment marks, and
persona notes keep their popovers anchored to the passage as the page
reflows.

**The Apparatus checks the draft instead of spotting missing footnotes.** It
now sweeps every category — quotations, named people in the context the draft
asserts, statistics, events, and checkable claims — rather than stopping at
the first. A citation already in the prose no longer ends the enquiry: a named
source is not proof that it says what the sentence claims. The Dossier's
declared format decides the discipline, so a novel is researched for
period and place authenticity while its invented plot is left alone.

**Steering the search reads like the rest of the room.** The steer box is the
same composer used elsewhere: the key sits under the text box, and a direction
can be spoken as readily as typed.

**The rubric returns a marked report.** Each grade carries its own stamp
impression and a short sounded acknowledgement, on proof paper.

**Exports can carry the margins.** Editors' marginalia can travel into DOCX
and notes exports, and stay out by default — persona feedback is private
working context until the writer opts in.

**Settings count properly.** Token budgets and temperatures use a real stepper
instead of bare number fields.

# App evaluation corpus

Drafts for running the real Twyne editorial room end to end. Every `draftText` is 550 to 900 words (the app refuses editor notes and rubric judgments under 500), counted as `text.trim().split(/\s+/).length`, except the `room-long-*` and `research.jsonl` cases described below. Every brief carries the full dossier shape the app expects. The table and eight-draft research description below record the original seed baseline; current totals after append-only expansion are **24 research, 24 rewrite, and 12 dossier cases**. The additions include two historical-fiction drafts, two zero-flag personal essays, all five rewrite editors at both levels (all 16 new cases preserve exact facts), and two zero-drift dossier controls.

## Plant kinds (`room.jsonl`)

`unsupported-claim`, `factual-error` (scholar), `weak-argument` (devil), `strong-passage` (angel), `bloat`, `repetition` (editor), `audience-gap`, `buried-lede` (reader, sometimes editor). `expectedEditors` is the set of editor ids a good run should see raise the passage; other editors may also comment.

## Long cases and the clamp (`room-long-*`)

The app now admits 48,000 characters (`DRAFT_CONTEXT_CHARS` and `clampForContext` in `convex/agentPrompts.ts`). Longer drafts keep the opening, ending and evenly spaced excerpts from the middle. The seven `room-long-*` cases retain plants near the beginning, middle and end; their original layout exposed the older 4,500-character opening/ending clamp. Middle plants in these cases now reach the editors, so they measure whether editors notice a visible problem rather than only whether it survives truncation. The scorer uses the product's current clamp to distinguish total and visible coverage. Positions are computed with `draftText.indexOf(passage)`; re-check them after edits.

## Verbatim rule

Every plant, research or drift `passage`, and each rewrite `original`, is an exact substring that appears once in its `draftText`. Each `mustPreserve` fact occurs in `original`; short terms can occur more than once. Passages use straight quotes and plain ASCII punctuation. Edit a draft and re-check its passages.

## Auto-research (`research.jsonl`)

Eight complete drafts cover a science explainer, history essay, policy memo, book review, technology feature, historical fiction, personal essay, and art criticism. There are 42 `mustFlag` targets across all six research kinds and 32 `mustNotFlag` targets. Each substantive factual draft has six required flags and four restraint targets. The personal essay intentionally has zero required flags and four restraint targets; an empty intake is correct there.

Run the same drafts through `prompts/research-extract-system.md` and the hosted strategy in `src/utils/research-candidates.ts`. The labels include rule triggers (figures, attributed quotations, studies, dated events), rule blind spots (works, person-context facts, plain claims, measurements), and tempting false positives (proposed percentages, private dates, invented dialogue). Fiction's brief identifies the invented characters and story; its historical scaffolding still needs sources. Statements about a published novel's contents are checkable literary claims.

Match a label when **all** lowercase `needles` occur in a target's anchor or all occur in its query. Each labeled passage appears verbatim exactly once. These are selected positive and negative labels, not exhaustive annotations: an unmatched target is not automatically wrong. For retrieval, search each `query` and judge whether the source supports or corrects the entire claim against `fact`; a matching `sources` domain or title fragment alone does not establish that it settles the claim. Preserve qualifications, editions, and object identities. Quote wording is grounded in [Project Gutenberg ebook 84](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/84/84-h/84-h.htm) and [NASA's landing transcript](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/a11.landing.html).

The expanded research suite has 126 `mustFlag` and 96 `mustNotFlag` targets. Four labels have `plantedError: true`, one per draft: `apollo-date` (1970 -> July 20, 1969), `austen-date` (1815 -> January 28, 1813), `voyager-launch` (1980 -> September 5, 1977), and `starry-year` (1888 -> MoMA catalogue June 1889); no other research error is intentional. Validate all three saved suites with `rtk proxy python /tmp/build_validate_twyne_research.py`; the extended read-only local script checks word counts, schemas, exact-once passages, lowercase needles and queries, `mustPreserve` inside `original`, globally unique IDs, coverage, and byte-identical original lines against the retained `/tmp/twyne-design-corpora-e1o2f_lp` snapshots. It does not run live extraction, rewriting, dossier judgment, or retrieval.

## What to build

### Pure analysis (src/utils/living-desk/)
Input: a lightweight segment model of the doc. Build it in a `segment.ts` that walks a ProseMirror `Node` into blocks `{kind: paragraph|heading|quote|code|other, text, pos (doc position of the block's content start), paragraph number, section index}` plus `sections: SectionInfo[]` split at headings (text before the first heading = "Opening"). Provide an offset→doc-position mapper that handles inline non-text nodes (see `posAtOffset` in struggle-tracker.ts). Keep the analysis functions taking plain data so they are testable without an editor. Skip quote/code blocks and text inside quotation marks ("…", “…”) for stance.

1. `stance.ts`: the I/we ledger.
   - Find first-person singular (I, me, my, mine, myself) and plural (we, us, our, ours, ourselves, let's) outside quotes/blockquotes/code.
   - The finding exists only when the piece is mainly singular: at least 3 singular and singular ≥ 60% of first-person tokens, and at least one plural.
   - Rule labels: inclusive ("as we'll see", "as we have seen", "let us/let's" + verb, "we can see"); group ("we" + a plural noun such as kids, children, students, villagers, "we both", "we all", "my family and I… we"); editorial ("we would argue", "we argue", "we contend", "we believe", "we must admit", "we think", "in our view", "our view", "our argument", "our thesis", "we have shown", "we will show"). Everything else is `unclassified` (shown, not flagged).
   - With Jev (when available): batch one Choice per unclassified-or-rule-labelled plural occurrence with options exactly ["the author alone (an editorial or royal we)", "the author and the reader together", "a group the author belongs to", "a character or quoted speaker", "people in general"]. State: the sentence, the previous sentence, and the counts of singular vs plural. At most 24 questions per request, cached by sentence text. Flag only when P(author alone) ≥ 0.6. Record the probability and a short note ("the author alone"). Jev overrides rule labels.
   - Deterministic fix per flagged occurrence, case preserved: we→I, us→me, our→my, ours→mine, ourselves→myself. Agreement with the immediately following verb: are→am, were→was, 're→'m, 've/'d/'ll stay, "we were"→"I was", "we are"→"I am". The occurrence span covers the pronoun plus the agreeing verb when that changes. Test all of this.
   - Finding id `stance`, lens `stance`, level `piece`, criterion `consistency`, title "Editorial “we” in an “I” essay", metric like `3 editorial “we” · 41 “I”`. Include context occurrences (flagged:false) for inclusive and group cases with a note.
2. `naming.ts`: capitalised name tokens and multi-word names not at sentence start. Group spelling variants: Damerau-Levenshtein ≤1 for length ≥5, ≤2 for length ≥8, same first letter, and the canonical form appears ≥2× as often. Exclude a stoplist of common capitalised words. One finding per group, id `naming:<canonical lowercased>`, lens `naming`, level `piece`, criterion `consistency`, fix = the canonical spelling. Title like `Hollins is spelled two ways`; metric `“Hollins” ×14 · “Hollis” ×1 in ¶6`.
3. `style-sheet.ts`: majority-rule consistency. One finding per rule that has a minority, fix = convert to the majority form:
   - serial comma
   - US/UK spelling pairs (a table of ~40 pairs, e.g. color/colour, center/centre, -ize/-ise, gray/grey, toward/towards)
   - spelled-out vs digit numbers below 10 (exclude dates, times, measurements, decimals)
   - spaced vs closed em dash
   - curly vs straight quotes
   Lens `style`, level `piece`, criterion `consistency`, provenance `rule`, ids `style:<rule>`.
4. `presence.ts`: entities = canonical names with ≥2 mentions. Build counts per section into `PresenceRow[]`. A finding when the doc has ≥3 sections and an entity present in ≥2 sections is absent from ≥1 section between appearances: id `presence:<name>`, lens `presence`, level `section`, criterion `structure`, impact null, actions ["jump"], occurrences = the last mention before the gap and the first after (flagged:false). Title e.g. `Mara Okafor drops out of §II` (use the section titles if short, otherwise §n).
5. `score.ts`: the live score.
   - Rule criteria from `scoreStaticFeatures(plainText)`: evidence, pacing, vocabulary, integrity, paragraphShape, structure. Use the labels from rubric.ts where they exist. scope `paragraph`, source `rule`.
   - Plus a `consistency` criterion = 10 − penalties: 0.3 per flagged stance occurrence, 0.4 per naming variant occurrence, 0.1 per style minority occurrence, capped total penalty 6.
   - Review criteria from `liveReviewSnapshot().review?.rubric?.judgementGrade` (scope piece, source review), updated on the `twyne:live-review` event.
   - Confirmed grade = the review's grade on a 0–10 scale (read rubric-grade.ts / types to convert correctly) plus the letter.
   - Estimate = confirmed + Σ w_k·(rule_k_now − rule_k_at_confirm) using normalised rule weights (reuse FEATURE_WEIGHTS where possible; give consistency a weight comparable to vocabulary). Without a confirmed read, estimate = the weighted mean of rule criteria. Clamp 0–10. Do not show an estimate under ~150 words: null.
   - `impact` per finding = weight(criterion) × (consistency if that finding were resolved − consistency now), computed by recomputing the penalty without it. `effort` = flagged count.
   - Sort findings: open by impact desc, null-impact after open, then improving, resolved, deliberate.
   - `delta` per criterion = value − value at confirm.
   - `lastChange` set after a fix: criterion with the largest change, its delta, and source `¶<n>`.
